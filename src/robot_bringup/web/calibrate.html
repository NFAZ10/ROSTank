Board

Count the inside corners, where four squares meet, not the squares themselves. Changing these clears the samples.

  • Prop the board up rather than holding it, and move it between shots. The two cameras capture about 14 ms apart, so any movement spoils the shot.
  • Cover the whole view (centre, all four corners and edges) between about 0.25 m and 0.6 m. With a small board, further away is too imprecise and gets refused.
  • Tilt it 30–45° left, right, up and down for some shots, no more, and keep the whole board in view.
  • No glare on the board, and keep it out of the robot's own body at the bottom of the view.
