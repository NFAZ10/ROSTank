Test drive

Buttons run only while held. Lift the robot or take the tracks off first.

What to look for

  • Turn a sprocket by hand and its count should move. One full sprocket turn is about 748 counts.
  • Forward counts up. Turning a track the way the robot drives forward should make the number rise.
  • Measured follows commanded. When a motor is held, the solid line should settle on the dashed one. Commanded but flat at zero means the motor isn't turning.
  • No count while turning? Check that encoder's plug, its 3.3 V and ground wires.
